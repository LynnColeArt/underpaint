Underpaint is an experimental AI photo restoration and image reconstruction workspace built from Drawpile.

The fork keeps Drawpile's collaborative editing foundation while exploring artist-directed restoration workflows, including layered masks, intentional generative fill, outpaint operations, model management, and inspectable AI results.

Underpaint is early-stage GPLv3 software. Drawpile provenance and inherited compatibility surfaces remain visible while the fork develops its own restoration-focused product identity.
